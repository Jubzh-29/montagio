# Demo assets

Files the `demo` backend (`MONTAGIO_BACKEND=demo`, no GPU) hands out instead of a render, and the sample photos, sample clip and starting faces that every backend offers. None is a model output, except the sample photos, the sample clip, the examples, the words of `captions/_default.json` and the faces below.

Before these files, the Studio tries two things (`apps/web/src/server/demo-render.ts`; `MONTAGIO_DEMO_RENDER=off` skips them, as the unit tests do):

1. a sample photo (byte for byte) with a preset a GPU session rendered on that photo gets that render: Crash Push or one of the five presets with two moves on the harbour (`presets/previews/`, 20-step renders: the caption names the Quality model, whatever model the preset now starts on), Squish or Frozen Moment on the dog (`presets/effects/previews/`), Wardrobe Swap on the portrait (`presets/transitions/previews/`), the avatar on the portrait and the motion on the character with the sample dance (`examples/`). The caption says a GPU made the clip from the same sample photo;
2. any other photo, for a job that makes a video, is moved by FFmpeg along the preset (a slow push-in for an effect, a hook, an avatar or a motion), at 480p, kept under `data/demo-renders/` and tagged `Montagio demo render`, not AI-generated. The caption says FFmpeg moved it.

| File | What it is | Made with |
|---|---|---|
| `clips/<preset>.mp4`, `clips/_default.mp4` | the clip of a camera move or an effect | pre-rendered placeholders |
| `clips/avatar.mp4` | the clip of an avatar, with sound like the real one (so a voice change can start from it) | `ffmpeg -i clips/_default.mp4 -i audio/_default.flac -map 0:v -map 1:a -c:v copy -c:a aac -b:a 64k -shortest -map_metadata -1 clips/avatar.mp4` |
| `images/_default.png` | the image of a text-to-image or edit job | `ffmpeg -f lavfi -i "gradients=s=1280x720:c0=0x1d2b3a:c1=0x6b3a2a:x0=0:y0=0:x1=1280:y1=720:speed=0" -frames:v 1 _default.png` |
| `audio/_default.flac` | the sound of a music or voice job | `ffmpeg -f lavfi -i "sine=frequency=440:duration=5:sample_rate=24000" -ac 1 -c:a flac _default.flac` |
| `images/sample-16x9.png` | the sample photo of the Studio: a fishing harbour at golden hour, the showcase's `landscape-a` (the camera previews show it), 1280×720, tagged AI-generated | Z-Image-Turbo (Apache-2.0), seed 42, from the prompt in `showcase/manifest.yaml`; a fictional scene, no real person |
| `images/sample-subject.png`, `images/sample-portrait.png`, `images/sample-character.png` | the sample photos of the Effects, Avatar and Motion tabs: a golden retriever on a lawn, a woman's portrait, a cartoon boy at full length; tagged AI-generated | Z-Image-Turbo (Apache-2.0), the GPU session S2's test photos (`s2-inputs/animal.png`, `face.png`, `character.png`); fictional, no real person |
| `videos/sample-dance.mp4` | the sample reference clip of the Motion tab, copied as it is (already 5 s, 16 fps, silent, like an imported clip once prepared); with a photo nobody imported, no rights to confirm; tagged AI-generated | Wan 2.2 TI2V 5B (Apache-2.0) from a Z-Image-Turbo photo, the dance of GPU session S2 (`20261021-131540-motion/inputs/motion.mp4`); no real person |
| `examples/avatar.mp4`, `examples/motion.mp4` | the full renders of GPU session S2 behind the muted previews of the Avatar and Motion cards: the portrait saying "Hello, this is a test of the Montagio avatar." (Montagio is the app's former name) in its voice, and the character performing the sample dance; tagged AI-generated | Wan 2.2 S2V (Apache-2.0) with the voice of Chatterbox Multilingual V3 (MIT), and Wan2.2-Animate-2 distilled (Apache-2.0), S2 runs `20261021-124523-avatar` and `20261021-131540-motion`; no real person |
| `captions/_default.json` | the words of a transcription job (captions): what Whisper heard in `examples/avatar.mp4`, each word with its moment. For another avatar, the demo spreads its typed text over its voice instead | Whisper large-v3 turbo (MIT) in the Montagio Transcribe Words node, run for real on a CPU ComfyUI on 2026-10-25 (`workflows/whisper-words.api.json`, the clip's sound as mono FLAC at 16 kHz), then on an L4 GPU the same day with the same words and moments; it heard "Shotrate" for "Montagio", and for an avatar the captions editor starts from the typed text instead |
| `captions/remix-skeleton.json` | the skeleton and the post of the trend remix's sample (three moves of the Motion library: Hip-hop, Wave and clap, Jumping jacks), as Gemma's two answers read by `parseTrendSkeleton` | written by hand for the demo from the library's own descriptions: no model ran; the post is a stand-in the user edits |
| `faces/<id>.jpg`, `faces/<id>.recipe.json` | the 28 starting faces (`apps/web/src/lib/character-bank.ts`): fictional characters the Characters page and the tabs that need a person (Effects, Avatar, Motion, the trend remix) can start from, 720×1280 JPEGs tagged AI-generated, each with the recipe of its render (`montagio/recipe@1`: the `/api/images` request with its text and seed, the workflow, the weights); their 240×320 WebP thumbnails are in `apps/web/public/characters/faces/`, and the static showcase shows 6 of them | Z-Image-Turbo (Apache-2.0) from the Characters tab's own text, one seed each, on an L4 (2026-10-25; five rendered again), ingested by `apps/web/scripts/character-bank.mts`; fictional, no real person |
| `showcase/` | the static showcase's manifest, sample photos and clips | see `showcase/README.md` |

A file named after a preset or an effect (`clips/<id>.mp4`, `images/<id>.png`, `audio/<id>.flac`, `captions/<id>.json`) replaces the default for that job. `MONTAGIO_DEMO_CLIPS`, `MONTAGIO_DEMO_IMAGES`, `MONTAGIO_DEMO_AUDIO` and `MONTAGIO_DEMO_CAPTIONS` point the backend elsewhere.

The clips of the Motion library are not here: they are in `presets/motions/previews/`, listed by `presets/motions/library.yaml`. The trend remix's example is cut from three of them when it is asked for.
